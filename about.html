About TravelWander

TravelWander is a travel recommendation company founded in 2024 with a simple mission: help every traveller discover destinations they will love. We combine curated local knowledge with smart search so that a single keyword can surface the perfect beach, temple or country for your next trip.

Our team of travel writers, photographers and destination experts work across the globe to keep our recommendations fresh, honest and inspiring.

Guidance for team members

To keep our recommendations consistent and high quality, every team member should follow these guidelines:

  • Keep destination data in travel_recommendation_api.json — add new entries under beaches, temples or countries.
  • Every recommendation must include a name, a working image URL and a short, factual description (1–2 sentences).
  • Verify that image links load before publishing, and prefer royalty-free sources.
  • Test the search for beach, temple and country after every change.
  • Commit small, descriptive changes and deploy via GitHub Pages.
